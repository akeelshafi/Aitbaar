Aitbaar Account Deletion

Aitbaar respects your privacy and gives users the option to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Aitbaar account, send an email to:

akeelshafi.dev@gmail.com

Please use the subject line:

Aitbaar Account Deletion Request

Include the following information:

  • Your registered phone number.
  • Your account role (Vendor or Customer).
  • Your request to delete your Aitbaar account.

Never include your password or OTP in the email.

2. Verification Process

For security reasons, we may request additional information to verify that you own the account before processing your deletion request.

3. What Information Will Be Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your Firebase Authentication account and eligible personal profile information associated with that account.

4. Transaction and Payment Records

Aitbaar maintains a shared transaction ledger between vendors and customers. Transaction and payment records may contain information about both parties.

Some historical transaction and payment records may be retained where reasonably necessary for ledger history, dispute handling, security, legal compliance or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Where appropriate, personal information in retained records will be removed or de-identified.

5. Deletion Confirmation

After processing your verified request, Aitbaar Support will send a confirmation email explaining the outcome and any applicable data retention.
