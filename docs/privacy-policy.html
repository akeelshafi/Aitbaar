Aitbaar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Welcome to Aitbaar, a digital ledger application that helps vendors and customers manage transactions and payment records. This Privacy Policy explains how Aitbaar collects, uses, stores and protects information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Phone number, name and account role.
  • Customer information: Customer name and phone number entered or selected by users.
  • Transaction information: Vendor and customer names, identifiers, item descriptions, transaction amounts, currency, status and timestamps.
  • Payment information: Payment amounts, associated vendor and customer details, and payment timestamps.
  • Technical information: Certain app usage and technical information may be processed through Firebase Analytics.

2. Contacts Permission

Aitbaar may request access to your device contacts to help you select customers conveniently.

Aitbaar does not upload your entire contact list to its servers. Only contact details that you select and use within the application may be stored as customer information.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Provide vendor and customer services.
  • Create and display transaction records.
  • Maintain payment histories and calculate balances.
  • Improve app performance and troubleshoot issues.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Aitbaar uses Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for user authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore for storing account, transaction and payment information.
  • Firebase Analytics, which is included in the application for app analytics.

These services process information to support the operation of Aitbaar. Their processing is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Transaction Information Sharing

Aitbaar is a shared digital ledger. Transaction information may be visible to the relevant vendor and customer so both parties can review their transaction and payment history.

We do not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Security

We use Firebase services and reasonable safeguards intended to protect user information against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Aitbaar accounts by sending an email to:

akeelshafi.dev@gmail.com

Please include your registered phone number and account role (Vendor or Customer). We may request additional information to verify your identity.

After verification, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account and eligible personal profile information.

Some shared transaction and payment records may be retained where reasonably necessary for ledger history, dispute handling, security, legal compliance or legitimate record-keeping. Where appropriate, personal information in retained records will be removed or de-identified.

For full instructions, visit our Account Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

Aitbaar is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
